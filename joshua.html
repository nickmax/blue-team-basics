Passwords and MFA

Four changes I made after a friend got locked out.

My password used to be my cat's name plus 123. I knew it was bad, I just never got around to changing it. Then a friend lost access to everything for two days and I fixed mine that night. These are the four changes I made, in the order I made them.

Four things I changed

Long beats complicated

A short sentence I can remember is safer than one messy word with an exclamation mark.

Never reuse one

One leak should not open five accounts. Every site gets its own password now.

Let the vault remember

I know two passwords. The password manager knows the rest and fills them in for me.

Turn on the second factor

Even if someone steals my password, they still need the code on my phone.
